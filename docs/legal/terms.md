## 1. What Ledger AI is

Ledger AI is a personal finance workspace. You upload bank statement files and
receipt images; it normalizes, categorizes and summarizes them, and answers
questions about them using deterministic queries over your own data.

It is a private beta, offered free of charge, to a small number of invited
people.

## 2. What it is not

Ledger AI is **not** a financial adviser, accountant, tax preparer or broker. It
does not connect to any bank or financial institution. It gives no financial,
investment, tax or legal advice. See `financial-disclaimer.md`.

## 3. Your account

Accounts are invite-only. Authentication is provided by Clerk, a third party;
your email address and sign-in credentials are held by them.

You are responsible for the security of the email account you sign in with,
because access to that mailbox is access to Ledger AI.

## 4. Your data

You keep ownership of everything you upload. You can export all of it at any
time, and you can delete it at any time.

You are responsible for having the right to upload what you upload.

## 5. Beta status — no guarantees

This is a beta. There is **no** guarantee of availability, durability or
correctness, and no service level of any kind. Data may be lost. Keep your own
copies of anything that matters.

Ledger AI may be changed or discontinued at any time.

## 6. Acceptable use

Do not upload another person's financial records without their permission,
attempt to access another user's data, or attempt to disrupt the service.

Do not upload unmasked full account numbers, Social Security numbers or similar
identifiers. The software attempts to detect and refuse these, but that
detection is best-effort and is not a guarantee.

## 7. Ending your use

You may delete your account at any time from Settings. Ledger AI may end your
access at any time, particularly during a beta.
