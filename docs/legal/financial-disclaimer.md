# Financial Data Disclaimer — DRAFT, REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW

**Version:** `2026-08-draft-1`

Ledger AI is a tool for looking at financial data you already have. It is not a
financial adviser and does not give financial, investment, tax or legal advice.

**Every figure is computed from the files you upload.** If a statement is
incomplete, so is the total. Ledger AI does not connect to any bank and cannot
verify that what you uploaded is complete or accurate.

**Alerts are statistical observations, not findings.** A charge flagged as a
possible duplicate or as unusually large is a pattern in your own data. It is
not a claim of fraud, error, or wrongdoing by anyone.

**Categorization is a best guess.** Categories come from deterministic rules
over merchant names. They are frequently wrong for unfamiliar merchants, which
is why every one can be corrected, and why anything uncertain is marked for
review rather than presented as settled.

**Currency is not converted.** Amounts in a currency other than your base
currency are reported separately and never added into a base-currency total.

Do not rely on Ledger AI for tax filing, lending decisions, or any purpose where
being wrong matters. Check the source statement.
